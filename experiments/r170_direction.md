## 1. Step 1 — Selection and justification

**One-sentence idea.** Give `kelly_regime_v4` a *meta-labeling* layer (López de Prado 2018, *Advances in Financial Machine Learning*, Wiley, ch. 3): keep the existing 3-anchor vote and conditional-volatility scale exactly as they are, and multiply their product by a learned, out-of-fold-calibrated probability that *this specific, already-decided trade* will be profitable net of the 0.40% fee — but, unlike this project's own prior attempt at the same literature (R-80), apply that probability strictly as a **post-`frac·scale` multiplicative layer**, never blended into the vote's own combination formula, and feed the classifier features drawn from data channels *outside* the close-price anchors the vote and scale already consume (funding rate, Deribit DVOL/VRP, volume-based illiquidity, and the trade's own episode-relative excursion), rather than the price/vote-derived features R-80 used.

### (a) Which constraint does it attack, and why "another indicator" is not the answer

It attacks **SIZE and ERR** jointly, in the specific compound shape this project's own standing diagnosis already privileges: SIZE because the multiplier's only job is to decide *how much* of an already-decided directional bet to hold, never what to hold or when to flip it (the vote is untouched, byte-for-byte, in both variants below); ERR because the multiplier is a formally out-of-fold-validated conditional probability (purged/embargoed cross-validation, permutation-null gated) rather than an ungated heuristic, closing exactly the gap the standing diagnosis names — "no error control anywhere in the signal path" — but on the *magnitude* decision, not (as every one of the eight prior ERR-axis constructions did — R-28/31, R-87 ×2, R-104, R-105 ×2, R-114 ×2, R-160, R-161/167 ×4) on the *timing* of a flip or a *tail-loss cap*.

It is explicitly not "another indicator": the classifier never outputs a buy/sell signal and never appears in the vote. It cannot generate a trade v4 wouldn't already have taken, and its output range is bounded to `[0,1]` so it can only ever hold *less* of v4's own directional call, never more and never the opposite side. This is the same "decide how much, not what happens next" shape as every profitable entry in section A (L-01 through L-06) and structurally unlike every one of the 20 registered INFO/no-attack losers.

### (b) Which ledger entries it is not a duplicate of

This is the most important question for this specific direction, because meta-labeling has genuinely been tried here twice before, and I want to name that plainly rather than let it hide.

- **R-04 (round 4, pre-`ROUTINE.md`).** Meta-labeling + triple barrier. Killed by a lookahead bug in the label construction ("their trend-scanning label looks forward and is inadmissible here"). Not a refutation of the mechanism, and R-80 already established that.
- **R-80 (08-21).** The real prior attempt, and the one this round is built to fix, not repeat. R-80's conservative branch fed a hand-rolled trailing hit-rate into R-53/R-55's confirming-vote formula (`frac=(anchor_sum+weight·meta_vote)/(3+weight)`) and failed because BTC's positive long-run drift makes even the bearish anchor bucket's trailing hit-rate look falsely favorable, diluting exactly the unanimous-bearish de-risk call that is v4's most robust property. R-80's novel branch built a real logistic meta-classifier (5 features, mostly price/vote-derived: vote value, vote persistence, a vol z-score, price deviation from the 40-day anchor, 5-day return) and *passed* its Step-A gate — out-of-fold AUC 0.5511 against a 500-draw permutation null's 95th percentile of 0.5288 (empirical p<0.002) — a small but genuinely real edge. It still failed at Step B, and R-80's own entry diagnoses *why*, precisely: because `meta_vote` was continuous rather than R-53/55's original discrete 0/1 external vote, and was combined the same additive way, `confirming_vote_frac` could no longer reach exactly zero (0% of post-warmup bars were flat at any tested weight, against v4's own 32.8%) — the mechanism silently destroyed v4's full-de-risk state regardless of whether the classifier's information was real. R-80's own closing line names the fix and leaves it undone: *"any future round reusing R-53/R-55's confirming-vote formula with a continuous... signal should either clip/floor the combination so `frac` can still reach exactly zero, or accept that it cannot."*

  This round takes the second option R-80 names — **accept that the confirming-vote formula cannot carry a continuous signal, and don't use it.** Instead of blending into the vote at all, the classifier's output multiplies the *already-computed* `frac·scale` product, pre-deadband. Because multiplication by anything in `[0,1]` preserves an exact zero (`frac=0 ⟹ frac·scale·m=0` for any `m`), v4's full-de-risk property survives by construction, not by tuning — this is the architectural fix, not a re-parameterization of R-80's mistake. It is also a factor-isolation move in R-62's own sense: R-80 modified the **vote/direction** factor; this modifies neither factor, adding a third, multiplicative layer on the **product**, the slot R-165 showed absorbs almost none of v4's own traded-path turnover on its own (a separate, useful fact carried forward into the falsification design below) but has never previously carried a *learned* (as opposed to hand-derived) input.

- **The "never-increase-only bounded-brake" family (R-34, R-41, R-53-conservative, R-73-conservative), 4-for-4 failed independent of feeding signal**, per the standing pattern this ledger has named explicitly. This is the closest structural relative to what is proposed here, and it deserves an honest reckoning rather than a hand-wave: a meta-label multiplier bounded to `[0,1]` **is** the same shape of object (never raises exposure above v4's own path). The four failed instances all shared one measured signature — an exposure-artifact R² > 0.99 against v4's own unmodified path, meaning the "brake" barely ever actually engaged and collapsed to something statistically indistinguishable from a flat rescale, regardless of what fed it (a posterior, a real basis magnitude, a macro stress z-score, a VRP). All four feeding signals were slow-moving, persistent external state variables that rarely deviate from a "neutral" level. A meta-labeling classifier is a different statistical object by design — it is fit specifically to discriminate, at each of v4's own historical rebalance events, whether *that particular trade* resolved profitably, which is a fundamentally more event-conditioned, higher-variance target than a slowly drifting macro z-score — but whether it actually escapes the collapse-to-flat-rescale pattern is an empirical question, not a given, and this design pre-registers the identical exposure-artifact R² check the four prior rounds used as a mandatory Step-0 kill switch (§2, both variants) rather than assuming novelty by construction. If it also collapses to R²>0.98, that is reported as the fifth confirmation of the same pattern, not reframed.
- **R-87 (Adaptive Conformal Inference on vote confidence / Kelly-scale dispersion)** and **R-160/R-161/R-167 (formal statistical guarantees on flip-timing / tail-loss)**: all four wrap a *coverage guarantee* around an existing quantity (a confidence interval width, a tail-exceedance rate). This round is not a coverage-controlling wrapper at all — it is a point-probability estimate from a supervised classifier trained on realized outcomes, closer in spirit to a forecasting model than an inference tool, and it is explicitly *not* claiming any formal Type-I control (that is precisely the axis R-160/161/167 already closed 3-for-3 for this architecture).
- **R-162 (Kaufman Efficiency Ratio, a hand-derived statistic of `close` alone)** and **R-141/R-163's tanh dampeners (hand-derived multipliers of price excursion)**: both are *a priori formulas* of quantities v4's own vote/scale already consume, which is exactly why R-162 failed the non-collinearity kill switch (R²=0.9998–0.99999 against v4's own path). This round's classifier is (i) *fit*, not hand-derived, on realized outcomes, and (ii) built primarily from data channels — funding rate, DVOL/VRP, volume/illiquidity — that are not inputs to v4's vote or scale at all, specifically to avoid re-running R-162's failure mode a second time on a different formula.
- **B-09 (conformal prediction, demoted)**: B-09's own standing diagnosis is quoted directly in R-80's entry — *"the binding problem is not that trust is miscalibrated but that correctly-calibrated trust is low."* This round treats that as the single most likely failure mode and pre-registers a test for it directly (the Step-A AUC-vs-permutation-null gate, reused from R-80's own validated design) rather than assuming the classifier has real skill.
- **R-100 / R-145 (funding as an INFO vote / as a venue-routing signal)**: both used funding as a *directional* or *financing-choice* input on the raw series; this round only ever uses funding, DVOL and volume as *features into a bet-confidence classifier*, never as a standalone vote or route — a different role for a data channel this project has already fetched and, in those other roles, already found wanting.

### (c) Is it simulable here?

Yes, with one disclosed methodological requirement and one disclosed data-coverage caveat.

- All classifier features are computable from already-committed 5-minute-or-coarser files (`btcusdt_perp_funding_8h.csv.gz`, `btc_dvol_daily.csv.gz`, the bar's own volume column) at or before the bar the classifier scores — no order book, no queue model, no data this project cannot already load.
- The classifier's **training labels** require a forward-resolved outcome (was this trade profitable H bars/days later) — this is standard triple-barrier/meta-labeling construction and is *only* used to fit the classifier on already-closed historical trades at each periodic refit point, with an explicit **purge + embargo** (this project's own `tradebot.inference.group_bounds`/`purged_train_mask`/`cpcv_splits`, already built for B-04 and already used by R-80) so that no label whose forward window has not yet resolved as of the refit date, and no label inside the embargo window around any evaluation fold, is ever used. At live/backtest-decision time, the model scoring the *current* bar is always the most recent one fit strictly on past, resolved labels — this is exactly R-80's own novel-branch construction, independently causality-probed there, and it is reused rather than re-derived.
- **Coverage caveat, disclosed up front**: real BTC perpetual funding data starts 2020-01-01, and DVOL starts 2021-03-24. Pre-2020 (and, for the DVOL feature specifically, pre-2021-03) bars fall back to a reduced feature set (volume/illiquidity and episode-relative excursion only) — the same fallback convention R-136 already used for DVOL and R-100 used for its cross-venue window. This shrinks the effective training set in the earliest years but does not block causality; it is reported, not hidden.

### (d) What would make me conclude it failed

Named now, before any code:

1. **The Step-0 exposure-artifact kill switch fires** (R² ≥ 0.98 of the candidate's `frac·scale·m` path against v4's own unmodified `frac·scale` path, on inner-train) — the fifth confirmation that a bounded `[0,1]` multiplier on this strategy, whatever feeds it, degenerates to a flat rescale.
2. **The Step-A gate fails** (out-of-fold AUC does not clear a 500-draw permutation null's 95th percentile, reusing R-80's own validated design) — meaning, per B-09's own standing diagnosis, correctly-calibrated trust is too low on this feature set to be worth carrying forward at all.
3. **It clears both gates but the inner-validation promotion bar (§2) is not met on both markets** — the ordinary default-REJECT outcome every SIZE-axis round here defaults to.
4. **It clears the promotion bar on BTC but the classifier's own coverage caveat (funding/DVOL start dates) is doing the work** — i.e., the entire improvement is concentrated in the DVOL-covered sub-window (post-2021-03), the same artifact R-136 flagged and disclosed for its own DVOL blend. This is checked explicitly, not inferred after the fact.

## 2. Step 2 — Design: two variants

Both variants modify **only** the slot between v4's existing `desired = frac · scale` computation and the existing 10% deadband/latch: `desired' = frac · scale · m`, where `m ∈ [0,1]` is the meta-label multiplier. The vote (`frac`), the conditional-volatility scale, the 1% band, the vol-regime hysteresis and the deadband/latch are byte-for-byte unchanged in both variants — matching R-62's factor-isolation discipline, now applied to a third, added layer rather than to either of the two existing factors.

Both variants share one **mandatory Step-0 gate**, run before any strategy backtest: (i) an identity-recovery check (`m≡1` must reproduce `kelly_regime_v4` exactly, bit-for-bit); (ii) the exposure-artifact kill switch named in 1(d)#1; (iii) the Step-A out-of-fold-AUC-vs-permutation-null gate named in 1(d)#2, run separately per variant since their feature sets differ. Failing (ii) or (iii) stops the branch before Step B, exactly as R-166's Step-0 gate did — no strategy code is run past that point for a branch that fails here.

### Variant CONSERVATIVE — literal multiplicative meta-labeling

**Mechanism, one sentence.** A periodically-refit logistic-regression secondary classifier estimates the causal, out-of-fold probability that v4's currently-held directional bet will still be profitable net of the 0.40% fee at a fixed forward horizon, and that probability linearly rescales the position size via López de Prado's own formula — so v4 keeps its full size only when the classifier is confident and shrinks toward flat, never toward the opposite side, when it is not.

**Exact modification.** A new, added multiplicative layer only (vote/scale/deadband untouched):
- **Label** (training only): for each historical bar where v4's vote *changed* (a genuine flip, not a deadband-absorbed micro-adjustment), was the forward `H`-day log return in the direction of the new position positive, net of the 0.40% fee, at the earliest of a fixed horizon `H` or the next opposing flip (a triple-barrier construction with the vertical/time barrier as PRIMARY and the two horizontal barriers set at ± the fee itself, so "profitable" already nets out round-trip cost).
- **Features** (causal, at the flip bar): funding-rate level and 30-day z-score (`load_funding_extended`), DVOL-minus-trailing-realized-vol VRP (falls back to a constant/neutral value pre-2021-03, disclosed), a volume-based Amihud illiquidity ratio (`|return|/dollar_volume`, trailing 20-day mean), and the episode-relative excursion since the *current* vote state began (favorable move in the current direction, ATR-normalized — R-163's own state variable, reused here as a classifier *feature* rather than, as R-163 built it, a hand-set multiplier). Four features, none derived from the close-price anchors v4's vote/scale already read.
- **Fit**: `sklearn`-free (this project's own house style, per R-80's novel branch: a hand-rolled logistic regression, matching `experiments/bayes_confidence.py`'s existing convention), refit on a purged+embargoed expanding window at a fixed cadence, `REFIT_DAYS ∈ {90, 180, 365}`.
- **Multiplier**: `m = clip(2·(p̂ − 0.5), 0, 1)` — López de Prado's own literal formula, unmodified.
- **Output**: `desired = frac · scale · m`, fed into v4's existing, unmodified deadband/latch.

**Falsification test.** ETH sign-replication (`data/ethusd_bitfinex_5m.csv.gz` via `scripts/build_bitfinex_dataset.py`) — this project's single most common and best-validated falsification device, and the correct default for a first attempt at a new mechanism shape. Kill condition: the primary config's inner-validation-equivalent ETH Δ-Sharpe (or Δ-log-growth) is opposite in sign to BTC's, or its 95% CI excludes zero on the *losing* side.

**Decision rule, numeric.** PROMOTE-CANDIDATE only if, at the frozen PRIMARY config (`REFIT_DAYS=180`, `H=10`), on inner-validation, **both** markets:
- paired stationary block-bootstrap (30-day blocks, 2,000 resamples) Δ-Sharpe ≥ **+0.20** (this project's standing ±0.2 noise floor, R-20) **or** a Δ-log-growth 95% CI that excludes zero favorably;
- risk-matched: exposure ratio and realized-vol ratio vs. v4 within ±10% (R-33's discipline) — if not matched, the cell is voided rather than scored, not silently counted;
- the ETH falsification test above does not fire.
Anything else is REJECT (default). This is a two-clause conjunction plus one falsification veto — every outcome maps to exactly one of {PROMOTE-CANDIDATE, REJECT}, with no third bucket to fall through.

**Noise-floor sanity check (ROUTINE Step 2's required two lines).** Comparable "one multiplicative factor added to `frac·scale`" rounds on this exact object (R-162, R-164, R-165) measured inner-validation paired daily-return standard deviations in the **0.015–0.03/day** range once the arms are risk-matched (a much tighter comparison than v4-vs-buy-and-hold's ~0.03/day, since both arms here share the *same* underlying trades and differ only in magnitude during flagged periods — closer to R-165's own comparable-family numbers than to R-78's full-strategy comparison). At sd=0.02/day, detecting the +0.20-Sharpe threshold at conventional power (t≈2) over a fixed 730-day inner-validation window implies a required daily mean edge of ≈0.000252/day but only delivers t≈0.34 at that n — **underpowered at fixed-n by roughly a factor of 6**, meaning `n≈25,000` days (≈69 years) would be needed for a naive fixed-n test to reach significance at this effect size. This mirrors R-78's own finding on a structurally different comparison and is disclosed rather than argued away: the stationary block-bootstrap used above is more powerful than this naive fixed-n calculation (it exploits path structure other than a single mean/sd pair), and every comparable round in this family has in fact used exactly that bootstrap rather than a fixed-n test — but the honest expectation, stated before any code runs, is that this test is well-powered only for a *large* (≥0.2 Sharpe) true effect within the available window, consistent with the base rate of roughly 30 prior SIZE-axis attempts on this object, of which zero cleared this bar. That base rate is the prior; this round does not get a lower bar for being ML-flavored.

**Grid / configuration count.** `REFIT_DAYS ∈ {90,180,365} × H ∈ {5,10,20}` = 9 core configs, + 1 identity check (`m≡1`), + 2 fee-tier reruns of PRIMARY (0.10%/0.40%) = **12 configurations**. Step-A's own permutation-null draws (500, reused from R-80's design) are the falsification test's null distribution and are not double-counted as strategy configurations, matching this project's standing convention (R-80 itself, R-166).

### Variant NOVEL — Hedge-blended multi-channel meta-labeling

**Mechanism, one sentence.** Instead of one classifier trained on all four features jointly, run four single-feature secondary "sizing experts" (funding, VRP/DVOL, illiquidity, episode-relative excursion) in parallel, each producing its own candidate `[0,1]` multiplier the conservative way, and combine them online with a no-regret multiplicative-weights (Hedge) algorithm — Freund & Schapire (1997, *J. Computer and System Sciences* 55(1)), already this project's own precedent (`hedge_experts`, R-130) — charged each expert's own realized contribution to fee-adjusted P&L, so a channel that turns out uninformative on this data is automatically down-weighted rather than needing to be pruned by hand.

**Why this is not L-09/L-17/L-18/R-130 again, stated precisely.** Every prior Hedge/regret-matching construction in this ledger blended *directional* experts, each capable of generating its own losing bet — L-09's own lesson is exactly "no-regret guarantees are relative to the best expert; when every expert loses to fees, so does the blend," and R-130's mixing weight was found measurably inert (an `r:=0` ablation reproduced its headline numbers). Here, no expert ever proposes a direction or a position: v4's vote is the single, unmodified source of direction and each of the four experts only ever outputs a `[0,1]` *shrink factor* on that one already-decided bet. A Hedge blend of shrink factors cannot manufacture a losing trade v4 didn't already take — its worst case is over-shrinking into low exposure (an exposure-artifact risk, guarded by the same R-33 risk-matching clause used in the conservative variant), not the L-09/R-130 failure mode of amplifying a bad direction. This is the "combine with something from outside mainstream trading literature" move Step 2 asks for, applied to a genuinely different object than this project's own prior uses of the same tool.

**Exact modification.**
- Four single-feature classifiers, each trained and refit exactly as the conservative variant's shared classifier (purged/embargoed, `REFIT_DAYS=180`, `H=10` fixed — not swept, to keep this variant's own grid about the blend, not a second hyperparameter search over the same space the conservative variant already covers), each producing its own `m_k = clip(2·(p̂_k − 0.5),0,1)`, `k∈{funding, VRP, illiquidity, excursion}`.
- A weight vector `w_k` updated once per day (not per bar, to keep the update cadence slower than any individual trade and avoid a spurious-precision online-learning artifact) by exponentiated-gradient Hedge: `w_k ← w_k · exp(η · r_k)`, where `r_k` is expert `k`'s own realized, fee-charged daily contribution (the fee is charged in proportion to how much that expert's own multiplier moved size relative to the blend, an explicit attribution rule frozen before any run) — reused verbatim from `hedge_experts`'s own existing weight-update code, not reimplemented.
- Combined multiplier: `m = Σ_k w_k · m_k` (a weighted average, which stays in `[0,1]` since each `m_k` does and weights sum to 1) — output feeds `desired = frac · scale · m`, into v4's unmodified deadband/latch, identically to the conservative variant.
- Learning rate swept, not fitted: `η ∈ {0.1, 0.3, 1.0}`.

**Falsification test.** `scripts/stress_test.py` Monte Carlo windows — chosen specifically (not the default ETH check) because this variant's own risk is path-adaptation instability: an online weight update is exactly the kind of mechanism that can look good on one realized historical path and fall apart under resampling. Kill condition: the primary config (`η=0.3`) is worse than v4 on Sharpe or max-drawdown in a majority (>50%) of the 24 Monte Carlo windows, or the *blend weights themselves* fail to converge to a stable, non-degenerate distribution (all mass collapsing onto one expert within the first year of every window would itself be reported as a finding, not silently treated as a pass).

**Decision rule, numeric.** Identical two-clause-plus-veto structure to the conservative variant, evaluated at `η=0.3`: Δ-Sharpe ≥ +0.20 or a Δ-log-growth CI excluding zero, on both markets, risk-matched within ±10%, **and** the stress-test falsification above does not fire (majority-of-windows failure or weight-collapse). Default REJECT.

**Noise-floor sanity check.** Same underlying object (`frac·scale·m`, m∈[0,1]) as the conservative variant, so the same 0.015–0.03/day comparable-noise range and the same conclusion apply: this test is well-powered only for a large (≥0.2 Sharpe) true effect within the 730-day inner-validation window; a naive fixed-n calculation at this effect size implies `n` on the order of decades, which the bootstrap's greater power only partially closes. Stated identically rather than re-derived, since the object being compared against v4 has not changed shape.

**Grid / configuration count.** `η ∈ {0.1,0.3,1.0}` = 3 core configs, + 1 identity check (uniform static weights, `η=0`, must reduce to the conservative variant's simple 4-feature average — an internal consistency check, not a free pass), + 2 fee-tier reruns of PRIMARY = **6 configurations**. The four experts' own individual Step-0 gates (identity check, exposure-artifact R², Step-A AUC-vs-permutation-null) are run once each and shared with the conservative variant's own per-feature diagnostics where the feature is identical — not re-run from scratch, and not double-counted.

**Round total.** 12 (conservative) + 6 (novel) = **18 core strategy configurations**, the number that would be carried into the deflated-Sharpe accounting at the program level if this round is dispatched — near the low end of this ledger's typical 12–52-config range for a two-branch round, deliberately kept small because both variants share one Step-0 gate and the novel variant deliberately does not re-sweep the feature-classifier hyperparameters the conservative variant already covers.

## Summary of what would make this round worth dispatching versus not

This is, honestly, the third attempt at meta-labeling on this codebase and the fifth-or-so attempt at a bounded `[0,1]` multiplicative layer on `kelly_regime_v4`. Both prior families have real, disclosed, named failure modes, and this design does not get to ignore either: R-80 found a genuine small edge (AUC 0.551) and then destroyed it with the wrong combination formula; the bounded-brake family found the *right* combination shape (multiplicative, on the scale factor) but fed it slow, persistent, ultimately near-constant external signals that never actually engaged. This design's only real claim to novelty is combining R-80's validated architecture-fix diagnosis (multiply post-`frac·scale`, don't blend into the vote) with the bounded-brake family's own missing ingredient (a fast, event-conditioned, *fitted* signal rather than a slow hand-set one) — and it says so, with both mandatory kill switches (exposure-artifact R², Step-A AUC gate) frozen to catch either failure mode from recurring before any promotion question is even reached.
